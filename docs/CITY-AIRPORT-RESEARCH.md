## Airport names and reference links

- **Nashik Airport (Ozar):** AAI aviation information lists HAL Ozar Airport, Nasik. The website page uses Nashik/Ozar wording. [AAI eAIP aerodrome information](https://aim-india.aai.aero/eaip/eaip-v2-05-2026/amendment/CoverSheet.pdf)
- **Mumbai:** Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport (CSMIA, BOM) and Navi Mumbai International Airport (NMI). The latter's official site identifies Navi Mumbai International Airport and provides its passenger and flight information. [Official CSMIA flight information](https://csia.in/flightinformation/passenger-flight.aspx); [Official Navi Mumbai International Airport](https://navimumbai.adaniairports.com/en)
- **Pune:** Pune Airport, Civil Enclave Pune Airport, Lohegaon. [Airports Authority of India](https://www.aai.aero/en/airports/pune)
- **Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar:** The Airports Authority of India airport page uses Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar Airport. [AAI airport page](https://www.aai.aero/en/airports/aurangabad); [Maharashtra Tourism district guide](https://maharashtratourism.gov.in/districts/chhattrapati-sambhaji-nagar/)
- **Shirdi:** Shirdi Airport, Shirdi. [AAI eAIP aerodrome information](https://aim-india.aai.aero/eAIP_Archive/21-03-2024/eAIP/IN-AD%202.1VASD-en-GB.html)

Airport codes used in the UI are short airport identifiers for enquiry navigation, not a claim about route/flight availability. Verify code/name mapping and nomenclature before launch, particularly if current airline or authority display names change.
