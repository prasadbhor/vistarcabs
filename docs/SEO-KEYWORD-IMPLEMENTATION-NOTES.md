## Important implementation rules
- Confirm the final target URL and title with the SEO executive before launch.
- Avoid duplicate pages with only keyword changes.
- Do not invent search volumes or guarantee rankings.
- Check each canonical URL, metadata description, internal link, sitemap entry and actual route.
- Business owners must verify cities/routes actually served, contact details, vehicle categories, fares and availability.
